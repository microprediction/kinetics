Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "Scalable Probit Share
Calibration," for publication in the Journal of Computational and
Graphical Statistics.

The paper gives an algorithm that calibrates a factor-structured
multinomial probit model -- recovering the utilities at which model
shares match observed shares, with the factor covariance fixed -- at a
thousand alternatives in three seconds on a laptop, with cost growing
linearly in the number of alternatives. The construction computes every
choice probability from one shared survival field in O(QNL) operations,
yields resampling-free Jacobian-vector products from the same pass, and
is certified to log-odds accuracy of a few parts in a hundred thousand
down to deep-tail shares. The well-posedness theory it relies on is
classical and attributed; the contribution is the algorithm, its
implementation, and its validation.

Every number in the paper is produced by committed, seeded scripts, and
a single manifest regenerates every table and figure; code and the
experiment suite are supplied as supplementary material. Given the
journal's lineage in this area (Genz, 1992, appeared in Volume 1), we
believe JCGS is the natural home for this work.

This manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. The author reports
no conflicts of interest.
